## 1. NATURE OF THE APPLICATION (ZERO-HOSTING ARCHITECTURE)

**StreamFlow is exclusively a client-side directory, search engine, and media player interface.**

* **No Video Hosting:** StreamFlow does **NOT** host, store, archive, rebroadcast, transcode, decode, re-transmit, or redistribute any media files, video streams, audio streams, or television broadcasts.
* **No Video Proxying:** StreamFlow does **NOT** run any media proxy, reverse proxy, stream forwarder, or caching layer. All video playback occurs strictly between the user's web browser and the third-party broadcaster's public endpoint via standard HTTP Live Streaming (HLS / m3u8) protocols.
* **No Content Ownership:** All logos, channel names, trademarks, and media streams belong to their respective copyright holders, broadcasters, and network owners. StreamFlow claims zero ownership over any third-party content.

## 2. SOURCE OF PLAYLIST METADATA

The channel listings, titles, and stream URLs displayed on StreamFlow are retrieved directly from the publicly accessible, open-source community repository maintained at:

```
https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/index.m3u
```

* The `iptv-org` project is an open-source, crowd-sourced catalog of publicly accessible television channels that explicitly prohibits pirated, subscription-based, or pay-walled streams in accordance with their repository guidelines.
* StreamFlow does not curate, author, verify, or maintain the playlist sources. Availability, resolution, content, and broadcasting hours are determined entirely by the independent third-party broadcasters.
