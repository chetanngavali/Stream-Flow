# PRIVACY POLICY

**Project:** StreamFlow  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/chetanngavali/Stream-Flow](https://github.com/chetanngavali/Stream-Flow)  
**Effective Date:** September 2026

---

## 1. COMMITMENT TO USER PRIVACY

StreamFlow is built with a **privacy-first architecture**. We do not harvest, track, sell, or monetize user personal data.

---

## 2. DATA COLLECTED

* **No User Accounts or Logins Required:** You can use StreamFlow without providing personal information, names, email addresses, or payment details.
* **Local Storage Data:**
  - **Favorites:** Channel IDs bookmarked by the user are stored strictly inside your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device.
  - **Watch History:** Recently viewed channel timestamps are stored strictly in `localStorage` on your local device.
* **Server Logs:** The backend server logs basic diagnostic HTTP requests (IP address, user-agent, endpoint) for standard DDoS prevention, rate-limiting, and error diagnosis. These logs are automatically discarded.

---

## 3. THIRD-PARTY VIDEO STREAMS

When you play a video stream:
* Your web browser connects directly to the third-party broadcaster's server.
* The third-party broadcaster may receive standard HTTP request headers (including your IP address and user-agent), subject to that broadcaster's own privacy policy.
* StreamFlow does not intercept or inspect this traffic.
